# Blueprint.33: warm brown clay with irregular dark exposures

Supersedes the staged Blueprint.32 palette. Removes the global lower-dark/upper-light ramp. The main clay body moves toward plain terracotta (one lighter material step than brown), retaining brown expanses and limited grey as the darkest material. Red/orange beds remain subordinate.

A continuous three-dimensional mineral field varies horizontally and vertically, with warped vertical levels and smooth interpolation between independent slices. Raised and lowered surfaces expose different parts of these volumes: dark patches can recur at any height, across broad areas, without a monotonic elevation gradient or block-by-block noise. Unequal depositional bed thicknesses and optional contrasting seams remain. The same shared geology supplies badlands, the arid stony-peaks path identified at X49878 Z24575, and DH previews. No new inference, terrain generation or map sampling is required by the patch field.

Checks: warm material dominance, substantial dark coverage at high and low elevations, limited grey coverage, non-monotonic variation with height, saved-blueprint material parity at the reported coordinates, and cross-section visual review. Diagram colors approximate unlit block colors; actual appearance depends on game lighting and shaders.

Existing chunks/LOD caches are not rewritten. Installation retains earlier releases as disabled backups.
